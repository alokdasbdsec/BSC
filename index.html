What we provide

Communication, security and network systems for your premises

We supply the equipment, install it on site, and stay on call to maintain it.

SVC-01

PABX

Private branch exchange systems that give every desk its own extension, with transfer, hold and a single company number.

SVC-02

IP-PBX

Internet-protocol phone systems over your office network, with IP handsets, softphones and room to add branches.

SVC-03

EPABX

Electronic PABX for small and mid-size offices: reliable intercom between extensions plus outside lines.

SVC-04

CCTV

    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the difference between PABX, EPABX and IP-PBX?

    All three connect many internal extensions to a few outside lines. A PABX or EPABX uses telephone cable and standard handsets. An IP-PBX runs over your computer network and supports IP phones, mobile softphones and multiple branches.

    Do you install systems outside Dhaka?

    Call us with your location and project size, and we will confirm availability and arrange a site visit.

    Can you maintain a system that another company installed?

    Yes. Tell us the brand and model, and our technicians will inspect, repair or upgrade it.

    Can my CCTV cameras be viewed on a mobile phone?

    Yes. Most of the recorders we install support remote viewing on a mobile app once connected to the internet.
